Welcome to Oakbridge Orthopedic and Family Hospital, where we are committed to restoring your health, renewing your hope, and providing comprehensive care for your entire family.

Our Services

Orthopaedic

Specialized care for bones, joints, and the musculoskeletal system, offering advanced treatments for injuries, arthritis, and orthopedic conditions to help you regain full mobility.

Pharmacy

A fully stocked in-house pharmacy providing convenient, fast access to prescribed medications, healthcare supplies, and professional guidance on drug usage.

Outpatient Care

Prompt, accessible medical consultations and treatments for general health conditions, minor injuries, and routine checkups without the need for overnight admission

Maternity

Compassionate, end-to-end care for expecting mothers and newborns, including prenatal care, safe labor and delivery, and postnatal support.
